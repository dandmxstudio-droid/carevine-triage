## KB-001: Resetting your password

**Category:** App & account
**Last updated:** 2026-06-12

If you forgot your password or the app says your password is wrong:

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address you used to enroll.
3. Open the reset email and tap **Reset password** within 30 minutes. Links expire after 30 minutes and can only be used once.
4. Create a new password with at least 10 characters.

**Still can't sign in?** Make sure you're using the latest version of the app. If the reset link says it has expired right away, request a new link and open it on the same phone where the app is installed. If that doesn't work, message Member Support and we'll reset your account manually.

## KB-002: Signing in on a new phone or with a verification code

**Category:** App & account
**Last updated:** 2026-06-12

When you sign in on a new phone, we send a 6-digit verification code to your enrolled email.

- The code arrives within 2 minutes. Check your spam or promotions folder.
- Codes expire after 10 minutes. Tap **Resend code** for a new one.
- If you changed your email address, the code goes to your old address. Contact Member Support to update it.

**Staying signed in:** Turn on **Keep me signed in** on the sign-in screen. If the app still logs you out every time you close it, go to your phone's settings and make sure the Carevine app is allowed to run in the background.

## KB-003: Readings not showing in the app

**Category:** App & account
**Last updated:** 2026-07-30

Your glucose meter and blood pressure cuff send readings over the cellular network. You don't need Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

Readings usually appear in the app within 5 minutes. If they don't:

1. Pull down on the Readings screen to refresh.
2. Check that your device shows the signal icon after a reading. No signal icon means the reading is saved on the device and will send automatically when signal returns.
3. Make sure your app is up to date.

Readings saved on the device are never lost. If readings are missing for more than 24 hours, message Member Support with the date and approximate time of the missing readings.

## KB-004: App crashes, blank screens or stuck loading

**Category:** App & account
**Last updated:** 2026-08-21

If the app crashes, shows a blank white screen or stays on the loading screen:

1. Close the app completely and reopen it.

3. Restart your phone.

## KB-006: Setting up your blood pressure cuff

**Category:** Devices
**Last updated:** 2026-04-15

Your cuff (model BP-50) comes ready to use with batteries installed.

1. Sit with your feet flat and your back supported. Rest for 5 minutes.
2. Wrap the cuff on your bare upper arm, about one inch above your elbow, with the tube running down the inside of your arm.
3. The cuff should be snug, with room to slide two fingers underneath.
4. Press **Start**. Stay still and quiet until the reading finishes.
5. Wait for the signal icon. Your reading is sent automatically.

For accurate readings, measure at the same times each day and wait 1 minute between readings.

## KB-007: Setting up your glucose meter

**Category:** Devices
**Last updated:** 2026-04-15

Your meter (model GM-300) is ready to use out of the box.

1. Wash and dry your hands.
2. Insert a test strip into the slot at the bottom of the meter. The meter turns on automatically.
3. Use the lancing device on the side of your fingertip.
4. Touch the drop of blood to the end of the strip.
5. Your reading appears in 5 seconds and sends automatically.

Your welcome kit includes the meter, 50 test strips, 100 lancets and a lancing device. If anything is missing, message Member Support and we'll ship it within 2 business days.

## KB-008: Meter error codes

**Category:** Devices
**Last updated:** 2024-11-03

Error codes for the GM-200 meter:

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E-1 | Strip inserted incorrectly | Remove the strip and reinsert it, contact end first |
| E-2 | Not enough blood on the strip | Use a new strip and apply a larger drop |
| E-4 | Temperature out of range | Move to a room between 50 and 104°F and wait 15 minutes |
| E-5 | Low battery | Replace the batteries |

For any other error, power the meter off and on. If it continues, contact Member Support for a replacement.

## KB-009: Cuff errors and inconsistent readings

**Category:** Devices
**Last updated:** 2026-04-15

**Cuff stops inflating or shows an error:** Check that the tube is firmly connected and not bent. Rewrap the cuff following KB-006 and try again.

**Readings vary a lot back to back:** Some variation is normal. Readings can differ by 5 to 10 points. Rest 1 minute between readings, keep your arm at heart level, and don't talk during the reading.

If the cuff shows the same error three times in a row, contact Member Support for a replacement.

## KB-014: Eligibility and job changes

**Category:** Billing & eligibility
**Last updated:** 2026-01-20

Your eligibility comes from your employer and is updated monthly.

- **Changed roles at the same employer:** Your eligibility may briefly show as inactive while your employer updates its records. This usually resolves within one billing cycle. Message Member Support if it lasts more than 2 weeks.
- **Leaving your employer:** Your access ends on the last day of the month your employer coverage ends. You do not need to return your devices. They're yours to keep, though readings will no longer send to the care team.
- **Got a notice that your coverage ended unexpectedly:** Message Member Support so we can check with your employer.

## KB-017: When to contact your care team, and when to call 911

**Category:** Health & safety
**Last updated:** 2026-08-01

Member Support can help with the app, devices, billing and scheduling, but **cannot give medical advice.** Questions about your readings, symptoms or medications go to your nurse.

**Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room right away if you have:**

- Chest pain, trouble breathing, or signs of stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, trouble speaking)
- Confusion, fainting or a seizure
- A very low blood sugar reading with symptoms that don't improve after treatment

**Message your care team the same day if you:**

- Have readings that are much higher or lower than usual
- Have new or worsening symptoms
- Missed doses of your medication or ran out

When a message mentions symptoms, readings or medications, Member Support sends it to a nurse immediately.

## KB-018: Reporting a medication change

**Category:** Health & safety
**Last updated:** 2026-08-01

If your doctor starts, stops or changes a medication, let your care team know so your nurse can update your care plan and watch your readings.

In the app, go to **Care Team > Messages** and send a message to your nurse with the medication name, the new dose and the date of the change.

A nurse reviews medication updates within 1 business day.
