Triage Tool: screens

18 artboards at 1280px, arranged as flows. Start at 0c. Read each row left to right; arrows name the click that moves you on. Everything is clickable: queue rows open tickets, "Back to queue" returns to 1a, the header switches queues, "Approve and send" on 2a goes to 2e, "Send to nurse" on 2a opens 2f, and "I'm on it" in the 4a banner goes to 4b.

Brand: actions Urgent / crisis Warning: outdated or missing article Triage Tool content Public Sans · IBM Plex Mono for ticket IDs

For the build: every Triage Tool value carries a data-field attribute naming its output key: crisis, clinical, category, subcategory, priority, route, confidence, language, member_mood, article_id, draft_reply, handoff_note, reason.
